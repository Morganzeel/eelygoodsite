#

I am delighted to share that after around 3 months of serious prep time, I achieved yet another major milestone -- as of yesterday, I am finally CCNA certified!\
<br>
<br>
![](https://postimg.cc/23Pb50tg)
<br>
<br>
This certification was a major undertaking, and it took a lot out of me, to be honest, but it was incredibly worth it.\
While I was familiar with a lot of the material, I also learned so much about computer networking and added many new skills to my toolbelt.I got to experiment with new technologies and already have ideas for upgrading and expanding my home lab, thanks to the equipment I acquired.\
My journey started on YouTube, like many others. Jeremy's courses and Wendell Odom's books built the foundation of my theoretical knowledge, and I couldn't reccommend them enough for anyone else pursuing their CCNA.\
For labs, I relied on the physical equipment I had with me, as well as Cisco Packet Tracer, which was incredibly useful.\
But above all else, the most important thing, in my experience, is willpower. I really had to give it my all this time, and I achieved some truly splendid results (>80% on almost all fields, even above 90% on a few!)\
I plan to pursue a CCNP and CySA+ in the near future, but for now, I think I'll try to apply for a few positions and see if I can land anything part-time. I might also upload some labs or CCNA-related content here on my website.\
Good luck out there! You've got this!
